The forward-order pair had combined callback rate 29.5% above baseline. The reverse-order pair had combined 70.3% above baseline. Combined repeated closely at 18.88 and 18.92 callbacks/s; baseline varied from 14.58 to 11.11/s. This confirms direction in both orders, while baseline variability makes the magnitude uncertain and leaves ambient/system-state effects unresolved. Every measured engine frame advanced by one; there were no skipped frame indices. Combined enables compute skin and dynamic shadow index, with flora instancing enabled. Baseline disables those two paths and sets `XRAY_METAL_E08_FLORA_INSTANCING_OFF=1`. All profiler, observer, color, oracle, and GPU-pass flags were absent in both arms.

Percentiles are nearest-rank values over per-callback `device:time_continual()` intervals.

The machine was on AC, with no recorded thermal, performance, or CPU-power warnings. Swap use did not change within any measured window: baseline 01 held at 3474 MiB; combined 01 at 3466 MiB; combined 02 at 3495.25 MiB; baseline 02 at 3508.75 MiB. Combined 02 system swap rose between the pre-run and post-run snapshots, but remained unchanged during its measured interval; this is an ambient system-state caveat, not a per-arm game allocation measurement. No gameplay save was written by the harness.
