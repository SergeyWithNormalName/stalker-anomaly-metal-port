# Local shadow consumer contract

This is a source map for interpreting `zero_deferred_receiver_samples`. It is
not a culling authorization and does not treat the older HOM diagnostic as an
authoritative inventory of light consumers.

## Production local-atlas consumers

- **Shadow production and record lifetime:** [MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:2321) creates the local R32 atlas and depth target. `renderLocalShadowBatch` writes one atlas tile and `R4LocalShadowAtlasRecord` per planned light/face ([same file](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:2771), record fill at 2814–2836). The atlas is overwritten batch by batch; the record vector describes the currently rendered batch until it is archived after its consumers.
- **Deferred local-light accumulation:** [MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:4492) binds the current atlas/records into `GBufferInputs`; the scene renders a shadow batch and accumulates its lights before moving to the next atlas batch (4551–4559). [MetalR4LocalLightAdapter.cpp](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4LocalLightAdapter.cpp:446) matches a shadowed light to the current record, validates it, and binds the local atlas in the shadowed accumulation shader resources (531–565). The final volume accumulation draw is at 576–579, after the two stencil-mask draws at 576–577. This is the consumer counted by the visibility observer.
- **Per-light volumetric accumulation:** The scene's volumetric frame gate requires a ready adapter, valid view/environment, and available volumetric shader source ([MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:4261)). When it passes, the scene iterates just-rendered batch records and calls `BuildMetalR4VolumetricSpotPass` (4562–4577) before archiving them. Its per-light admission contract is in [MetalR4VolumetricCapture.cpp](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4VolumetricCapture.cpp:90): local volumetrics enabled, mode not 1, SSFX or per-light enable, shadowed Spot/OmniFace, and not HUD. It validates identity/frame/face and rendered depth at 113–115. Accepted spot passes retain the local atlas; [MetalR4VolumetricAdapter.cpp](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4VolumetricAdapter.cpp:574) binds it at texture slot 48 for the spot-volume draw (649–655). Thus a zero deferred count does not cover active per-light volumetric slices. The separate sun-volume path binds a sun shadow map, not this local atlas.

The optional local-batch witness and isolated local-shadow probe also read records or render from their matrices ([MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:4580), 4761–4805). These are diagnostics, not runtime consumers that can authorize suppression.

## Other passes and current uncertainty

- **Forward/translucent:** The production scene has static forward/lightplane and effects passes ([MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:5258), 5296–5345, 5408–5416). A source search of non-test `xrRenderMetal` code found no local-atlas/record binding in those adapters; the direct local-atlas references are the deferred and volumetric paths above, plus diagnostic witnesses. This is scoped source evidence, not proof that equivalent engine-side light data is unused outside Direct Metal.
- **HUD:** Local-light capture preserves `hudMode` and transforms HUD light position/direction ([MetalRenderDeviceRender.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalRenderDeviceRender.mm:1056)); the same snapshot is consumed by the deferred local-light adapter, which does not branch on `hudMode` before accumulation. Separately, HUD geometry is drawn from `R4EngineHudSnapshot` in its own pass ([MetalR4EngineScene.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalR4EngineScene.mm:4175), 4219–4228) and the HUD adapter has no local-atlas input. The volumetric builder explicitly excludes HUD lights. Any broader HUD-light consumer semantics remain unproven; the observer currently excludes `hudMode` groups conservatively.
- **SVP/secondary view:** `xrRenderMetal` has no `IsSVPFrame`/`SecondViewport` path in the source search. The normal Direct Metal route calls `scene.Render` for the admitted primary view ([MetalRenderDeviceRender.mm](../../../src/Layers/xrRenderMetal/MetalRenderDeviceRender.mm:1496), 1540); the only second call found is a temporal lifecycle probe that expects rejection before encoding (1600–1613). The D3D device renderer has a separate `m_SecondViewport` presentation gate ([dxRenderDeviceRender.cpp](../../../src/Layers/xrRender/dxRenderDeviceRender.cpp:410)); this does not establish whether any external caller can route a secondary view through Direct Metal. That boundary needs engine-call-site confirmation before suppression.

## Suppression preconditions still unresolved

Any future current-frame suppression would need evidence that every intended consumer for the selected stable light identity was covered in that frame: all six valid omni records (across batches) or the single spot record; deferred accumulation completion; and, whenever the admission gates above pass, the volumetric draw's required position/atlas samples. HUD treatment and any secondary-view or non-Metal engine route need an explicit caller contract. Until those are resolved, `zero_deferred_receiver_samples` means only that the counted deferred accumulation draw had no depth/stencil-qualified receiver samples; it does not mean hidden, invisible, or no total-frame contribution.
