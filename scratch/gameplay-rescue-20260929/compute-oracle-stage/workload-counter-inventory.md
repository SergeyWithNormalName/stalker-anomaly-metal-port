The frame-327 counter lines are `METAL_LOCAL_LIGHT_FRAME`, `METAL_LOCAL_SHADOW_FRAME`, and `METAL_E08_STATIC_MERGE` in `runs/v5-compute-skin-attribution-01/output/engine.log`. Static-sun and DefVertex records are in the same log. The frame-level dynamic-world counters are in `runs/v5-compute-skin-attribution-01/output/e07-dynamic-world.csv`; object snapshots are in `e07-dynamic-world.csv.objects.csv`.

Coverage gap: this attribution run enabled the production dynamic-shadow index but intentionally left the expensive oracle off. Its log therefore has no per-frame dynamic index view/fallback/miss counter. Earlier V4 live correctness reported 282,000 indexed shadow views, zero fallback views and zero misses (final samples in [`v4-depth-oracle-01/output/engine.log`](../runs/v4-depth-oracle-01/output/engine.log), e.g. frame 995: 4,512,673 candidate checks); that is a separate correctness receipt, not a V5 timing-run counter. Neither log exposes a complete unique local-shadow parent-to-face/view breakdown or actual per-draw duration. Do not infer either from candidate, caster, draw, or batch totals.

No times are allocated across the rows above. E07's `skin_ms`, `bounds_ms` and `skin_upload_ms` in the linked V5 attribution are CPU fallback stage timers; whole `dynamic_prewarm_ms` is the applicable CPU boundary for the newly integrated compute bounds path. Timing distributions remain in `attribution-analysis.md`.
